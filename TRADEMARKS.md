# ZotLit Trademark Policy

Version 1.0, 2026-11-26.

The ZotLit source code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License](LICENSE). The ZotLit name and logos are trademarks of AidenLx. The license gives you rights to the code, and this policy tells you how to use the name and logos. Under section 7(e) of the license, the license grants no rights to them.

The goal of this policy is simple. When a researcher sees the ZotLit name, they get the software and services that AidenLx publishes.

## The marks

- The name **ZotLit**, in any capitalization, and names that are confusingly similar to it.
- The ZotLit logos in [`assets/logo/`](assets/logo/): the ZL mark, the tile, and the wordmark. The [brand specification](docs/brand.md) describes them.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Refer to ZotLit.** Use the name to talk about ZotLit, write about it, review it, or teach it.
- **Describe compatibility.** Say that your plugin, template, or tool "works with ZotLit" or is "for ZotLit". Make sure that its own name stays different from ZotLit.
- **Share official releases.** Share an unchanged official release with its name and logos. The official releases are on the ZotLit website, in the Obsidian community plugin directory, and on the [releases page](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/releases).
- **Link to ZotLit.** Use the logo to link to the ZotLit website or repository.

## Uses that need permission

Ask before you use the marks in these ways:

- **A changed version.** If you distribute a changed version of ZotLit, give it a different name, a different Obsidian plugin ID, and a different logo. You can say that it is "based on ZotLit".
- **A hosted service.** Use the ZotLit name or logo for a hosted service, a paid service, or a set-up service only with permission.
- **Names and accounts.** Put the name in a product name, company name, domain name, or social media account only with permission.
- **Merchandise.** Put the name or logos on items for sale only with permission.
- **Endorsement.** Imply that AidenLx endorses or supports your product or service only with written agreement.

Send your request to aiden.lx@outlook.com. Include the name or logo you want to use and how you plan to use it.
